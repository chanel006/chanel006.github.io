Privacy policy

Video upload tool · last updated 4 November 2026

What the tool is

This is a personal tool used only by its owner. It uploads the owner's own videos, thumbnails and text from the owner's own server to the owner's own YouTube channels.

What data it accesses

  • Only the owner's own YouTube channels, through the YouTube Data API: their videos, playlists and scheduling.
  • It does not read the owner's email, contacts, calendar or any other Google data, nor data of any other person.

What it stores and shares

  • The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the owner's server, so uploads can continue.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service.

How to remove access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
